Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

Account Information
When you register or are added to TaskFlow, we collect your full name, email address, and role within your organization. Profile photos are optional and uploaded at your discretion.

Task-Related Information
We collect data related to tasks assigned to you, including task details, status updates, completion timestamps, notes you submit, and any photos or files you upload as completion evidence.

Device Information
We collect your device’s push notification token to deliver task assignments and updates to your device. This token is associated with your account and updated automatically when it changes.

Location Information
TaskFlow does not collect your real-time location. If a task includes a location field, that location is entered by the administrator as part of the task description, not tracked from your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

Deliver and manage tasks assigned to you
Send push notifications for new assignments, updates, and deadlines
Allow administrators to review task completion evidence
Maintain your account and profile

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your information is accessible only to administrators and managers within your organization, and to TaskFlow’s technical team for platform operation and support purposes.

4. Data Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Authentication is handled through short-lived JWT tokens. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data against unauthorized access.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active within your organization. Uploaded files and task records are retained for audit and record-keeping purposes according to your organization’s policy.

6. Third-Party Services

TaskFlow uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) to deliver push notifications. Firebase may process device token data as part of this service. Please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy for details.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting your organization’s administrator or reaching us directly at privacy@taskflow.app.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions:
Email: privacy@taskflow.app
