About TaskFlow

About Us

Who We Are

TaskFlow is a professional task execution and workforce management platform built to streamline how teams assign, track, and complete field work. We connect administrators and managers with their on-ground workforce through a seamless digital experience — eliminating paperwork, missed assignments, and communication gaps.

Our Mission

To empower organizations with the tools they need to manage their workforce efficiently, transparently, and in real time — from task creation to completion evidence.

What We Do

TaskFlow gives administrators full visibility over their team’s workload. Tasks are created and assigned through the web dashboard, instantly delivered to workers on their mobile devices, and tracked through every stage — from assignment to completion.

Workers receive clear instructions, deadlines, and requirements directly on their phones. They can start tasks, complete checklists, upload photos and files as evidence, and submit their work — all from a single app.

How It Works

An administrator creates a task and assigns it to a worker
The worker receives an instant push notification
The worker opens the task, reads the instructions, and starts working
The worker submits completion with notes, photos, or files
The administrator reviews the completed work on the dashboard
